I'd been wanting to ride around the
[area between Palmer Lake and Larkspur Colorado](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Yc6T3MTfmwMtRqDTA)
for some time and finally did on August 15, 2026 with a 32 mile ride
from the [Baptist Road trailhead](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XZ1GXugqeQ1RjBgJ6) in
Monument north through [Palmer Lake](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9cDEtVQbkFjJ4j5N7)
and [Greenland Open Space](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MtkEoQyYkJhmpMNn8) to
[Larkspur](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DKqQ9CM6ew2a39Mx5), then back. It was one of
the more unique and memorable rides I'd done at that point.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1411.jpeg %}

I'd rode on the [New Sante Fe Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/colorado/santa-fe-regional-trail)
through the US Air Force Academy to Baptist Road once, and short of that several
times, but I hadn't been North of Baptist Road on the trail. Unfortunately, the
trail was closed on the South end of the Air Force Academy in the Spring of
2026, so I hadn't been able to travel through it on my usual rides.

The trail throughout the Greenland Open Space, and especially North of there
towards Larkspur, was a lot more primitive and less developed than the trails I
was used to at that point, but it was a welcome change of surface. There were
long views across terrain that felt distinct from what I'd been riding farther
south.

Greenland Open Space had a surreal, almost liminal feel. It was quiet and open,
with plenty of solitude. Cattle grazed the land and were among the trails,
though I wasn't near any throughout my ride.

{% photos 2026/IMG_1414.jpeg 2026/IMG_1423.jpeg %}

North of Greenland, the trail to Larkspur became rougher and looked like it
didn't see much traffic. I passed one couple. There was thick mud, deep ruts,
tall grass, and rocky terrain along the way. We recently had a ton of rain,
which made the landscape more green but the trails very muddy in sections. Even
with I-25 running nearby between Colorado Springs and Denver, it felt lonely and
isolated.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1427.jpeg %}

On the way back through Greenland, I passed an area of pale rock formations
that reminded me of an uncolored version of the [Paint Mines Interpretive
Park](https://parks.elpasoco.com/parks-and-recreation/paint-mines-interpretive-park/)
east of Colorado Springs.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1454.jpeg %}

{% photo 2026/IMG_1466.jpeg %}

The rougher stretches were hard on my body, and I was thoroughly tired by the
time I got back. Still, I enjoyed the scenery and the unique landscape. It was
a demanding ride, but one that was fully satisfying and memorable. I'll
definitely go back out there again.

{% photos 2026/IMG_1438.jpeg 2026/IMG_1471.jpeg %}
