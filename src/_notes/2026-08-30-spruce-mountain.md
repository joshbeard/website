A walk around [Spruce Mountain](https://www.douglasco.gov/open-space-natural-resources/properties/spruce-mountain-open-space-and-trail/) near Palmer Lake, Colorado.

Really a cool hike and not very busy on the morning I went, though I was there shortly after sunrise. My total was just under 6 miles and it was pretty easy. I'd like to come back and run this one.
