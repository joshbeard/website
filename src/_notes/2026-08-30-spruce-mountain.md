I'd been eager to do a hike, something I did quite a bit of a few years back (see
[/photos](/photos/)), but wanted low-commitment and something close by. I
narrowed it down to [Spruce
Mountain](https://www.douglasco.gov/open-space-natural-resources/properties/spruce-mountain-open-space-and-trail/)
near Palmer Lake, Colorado. I recently did [32 miles through the nearby
Greenland Open Space](/notes/2026/greenland-open-space/) and had great views of
Spruce Mountain along the way.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1548.jpeg %}

I got there shortly after sunrise. It was overcast, which made the morning even
better. Spruce Mountain is mostly forested, and the trail mostly follows the
perimeter of this flat-topped little mountain. Hardly any climb - more of a
casual walk. The cliffs were really scenic, and the distant views were nice
too. I saw a couple of large deer with huge racks while I was up there.
Encountered a handful of people along the way, but it was still mostly quiet,
and I enjoyed the solitude.

My total distance was just under 6 miles, and it could probably be extended a
little. I'd like to come back and run this one.
