Milestone bike ride - my longest ride to date.

I'd been wanting to do a 50 mile ride all summer but just hadn't gotten around to it. One issue I have is that riding back home is an annoying climb back for a while, with the worst of it being in the last mile. So long rides get a bit painful at the end if I'm riding from home and back.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1598.jpeg %}

I wanted to do something in town and knew I could get the mileage out of the Pikes Peak Greenway and the Fountain trail, with what little was open of the New Sante Fe trail. I parked at the [Goose Gossage ball fields](https://maps.app.goo.gl/oA6EWN4qKnkWrKZv7) and headed South, as far as I could, on the Pikes Peak Greenway trail and into the Fountain Creek Regional Trail. The trail comes to an abrupt and unceremonious [end](https://maps.app.goo.gl/b6DqMzmcz6TPttf87) at a closed-off bridge.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1602.jpeg %}

{% photo 2026/IMG_1604.jpeg %}

I thoroughly enjoyed the scenery on this, except for the sketchy and gritty [couple of blocks under overpasses in town](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ofp8QashqJeupZSt7). I'll definitely do it again.

I listened to Pink Floyd, atmospheric drum n bass, and Ween on this ride.
