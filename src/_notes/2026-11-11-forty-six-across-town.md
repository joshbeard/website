It's been a few weeks since I did a longer ride - something over 30 miles - and
I've been eager to do one. I was sick a couple of weeks ago and have had family
commitments the past few weeks. The time commitment of a long ride isn't
trivial, either. Since I had Veteran's Day off, I took advantage of it and the
beautiful weather to do a long ride.

This ride was just over 46 miles - my second-longest ride yet, apparently. I
didn't set out that morning with the intention of doing my second longest ride -
if I knew I was that close, I probably would've planned more and beat [my
personal record of 50 miles from a couple of months ago](/notes/2026/fifty-miles-on-the-greenway/). I also would've started a lot
earlier. I didn't get started until after 9am and figured I'd spend maybe two
and a half hours out. Three tops. It turned into around four hours out - the
ride plus some stops to admire scenery and snap a few photos.

I started on the singletracks along Cottonwood Creek, then took the New Santa Fe
Trail into the Air Force Academy for a couple of miles. Unfortunately, that's
still partially closed for utility work a couple of miles in.

From there, I turned around and continued on to the Pikes Peak Greenway to
Sinton Trail and climbed to [Mesa
Overlook](https://maps.app.goo.gl/8j2UH7uS68y3w4Lk8) for views of Garden of the
Gods and Pikes Peak.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1964.jpeg %}

I continued south through Mesa Valley Trail, rejoined the Greenway, and headed
to Bear Creek Regional Park, where I lost my sunglasses and spent _a long time_ looking for them, before making my way
home via the Greenway and Cottonwood Creek Trail.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1975.jpeg %}

{% photos 2026/IMG_1989.jpeg 2026/IMG_1992.jpeg %}

I've done this basic route and similar variations before, and you can extend it in multiple ways to really hit some great spots in the area.

A long loop connecting familiar stretches with a few places I don’t reach on an
everyday ride, with plenty of stops for photos along the way.
