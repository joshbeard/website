It's been a few weeks since I did a "longer" ride - something over 30 miles - and I've been itching to do it. I was sick a couple of weeks ago and have had family commitments the past few weeks, along with some bad weather conditions. Since I had Veteran's Day off, I took advantage of it and the beautiful weather to do a long ride.

This ride was just over 46 miles - my second-longest ride yet, apparently. I
started on the singletracks
along Cottonwood Creek, then took the New Santa Fe Trail into the Air Force
Academy for a couple of miles (still partially closed for utility work). From
there, I turned around and continued on to the Pikes Peak Greenway to Sinton
Trail and climbed to Mesa Overlook for views of Garden of the Gods and Pikes
Peak.

{% photos 2026/IMG_1955.jpeg 2026/IMG_1958.jpeg 2026/IMG_1960.jpeg 2026/IMG_1962.jpeg %}

Unfortunately, I got started late and the ride ate up the entire middle part of the day, changing lunch plans.

{% photo 2026/IMG_1964.jpeg %}

I continued south through Mesa Valley Trail, rejoined the Greenway, and headed
to Bear Creek Regional Park, where I lost my sunglasses and spent _a long time_ looking for them, before making my way
home via the Greenway and Cottonwood Creek Trail. A long loop connecting
familiar stretches with a few places I don’t reach on an everyday ride, with
plenty of stops for photos along the way.
