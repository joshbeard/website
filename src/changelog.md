## Changelog

Notable changes to my website.

### November 15, 2026: Changelog

Add a changelog page.

### November 14, 2026: Notes Section

I added a [Notes](/notes/) section. I've avoided a blog, but I've wanted a
place to write a little about outings and group some photos with the notes. I
back-filled a few recent bike rides. Not sure how much I'll use it. It isn't a
ride report, and it isn't only for biking or hiking.

### November 7, 2026: Feed

I added a [feed](/feed.xml). It started with new photos. Notes joined it when
that section went up a few days later.

### October 29, 2026: Social

I added a [Social](/social/) page. Email is how to reach me, and I don't use
social media to keep in touch. The same day, I added a page for the
[latest photo](/photo/).

### July 28, 2026: Photos and workouts

I started linking photo captions to the workout they came from.

### July 2, 2026: Health

I added a [Health](/health/) page. It's a short record of weight, labs, and
what changed. The [workout log](/workouts/) is the detail.

### June 23, 2026: Workouts

I added a [Workouts](/workouts/) page, generated from my workout export.

### June 17, 2026: Photo albums

Photos are one album per year now, back to 2014. I folded the separate trip
albums (Herman Gulch, the 2019 road trip) into those years. The gallery
itself dates to April 2022. I filled in 2022-2025 in November 2025.

### January 16, 2026: joshbeard.com

I moved the site from joshbeard.me to joshbeard.com.

### April 19, 2022: Photos

I added a [photo gallery](/photos/). Year albums, plus a couple of trip
albums.

### March 28, 2022: A small site

The one-page link list grew up over the next couple of weeks: [Me](/me/),
[About This Site](/site/), [Now](/now/), [Uses](/uses/),
[Homelab](/homelab/), [Old Computers](/old-computers.html), and a
[PGP](/pgp/) page (that one was a few days earlier). Gemini, Gopher, and
Finger became the [Small Internet Stack](/site/small.html) page.

A posts section went up the same week and came down on April 20. The only
post was that small-stack writeup.

### January 20, 2021: Jekyll

I rebuilt the site with Jekyll and started deploying it to S3 and CloudFront.
It was still mostly a front page.

### June 10, 2020: joshbeard.me

I launched the site on `joshbeard.me`. A landing page under my real name, after
years of hobby sites on various other domains. This was a fresh start. The
page itself was a short list of links: email, GitHub, and LinkedIn.
