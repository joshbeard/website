## Health

{% include submenu.html %}

For years, an increasingly sedentary lifestyle, work, and habits were taking a
toll. My weight and lab results were heading in the wrong direction, and in
mid-2025 my Apple Watch began warning me about a rising resting heart rate. By
the grace of God, that was the push I needed to make some significant and (so
far) lasting changes.

This page is a record of those changes and what I'm doing now. Keeping it
public gives me some accountability, and I hope it might encourage someone
else. My detailed activity log is on the [Workouts](/workouts/) page.

What it's come down to for me is the basics, the obvious - calories in, calories
out. These days I log my food, count calories, and exercise.

### Getting Started

I started by committing myself to "incidental" exercise by bicycling a ~1.5 mile
round trip to my neighborhood 7-Eleven for my nicotine vice at the time. I
decided to make it easy - I wasn't going to be drastic and try to change
everything at once. I figured if I was going to get nicotine, I'd bike to do it.

Over the next few weeks, those bike rides started to get a little longer and
independent of the gas station runs. I started doing loops around my
neighborhood, progressing to 2-3 times a day. A couple months later, I started
taking rides outside my neighborhood using the city trails. I'd do a quick
neighborhood loop on breaks at work and a longer one over my lunch break.

I've continued to do daily lunch break rides, keeping them under an hour. I'll
occasionally do an after-work ride instead, and will often make those longer.
On weekends, I usually try to plan a longer ride - something like 25-40 miles,
and beyond that a few times now.

I really don't know what made this stick for me. I've had a few attempts to pick
up exercise routines in the past, but they didn't last long. I hiked quite a bit
for several years, but it wasn't frequent enough to make the sort of impact I
needed.

### One thing led to another

Initially, I decided that I wasn't going to worry about my diet or anything
besides doing daily bike rides in the neighborhood. I wanted to treat it as if I
_had_ to cycle for a commute or something.

It didn't take long for me to get the needed gumption to make other changes. I
quit using nicotine after using it for most of my adult life up to that point.

It also didn't take long to start being more intentional about how and what I
was eating. Not huge changes, just more compatible with my goals. I didn't weigh
myself until a month or so into the new cycling habit. I was almost too afraid
to know. It wasn't until five to six months into it that I locked in and
started logging my meals, counting calories, and trying to stick to a budget.
That was in December 2025. I also started to add in more variety with
dumbbells and walking.

In the late summer of 2026, I started running a bit.

### What's changed so far

It's been nearly 18 months since I got that heart rate alert from my Apple
Watch, and everything has moved in the right direction:

- **Weight:** Down 70 lbs and close to my target.
- **Cholesterol:** total cholesterol is healthy
- **Resting heart rate:** from the low 70s when I started tracking closely to
  the mid-to-upper 40s.
- **Fitness:** VO2 max is up to "above average" and steadily increasing.
- **Consistency:** 700+ logged workouts since mid-2025, mostly cycling.
- **Sleep:** better overall, though the new puppy has temporarily disrupted it.
- **General feel:** better posture, mood, confidence, and day-to-day energy.
- **Focus and productivity:** have significantly improved.

### What I'm doing

**Exercise:**

I've built a solid fitness base and working to keep markers like A1C,
cholesterol, and blood pressure in the normal range.

- **Cycling:** the main thing, and the first routine that has really stuck. I
  bought a new bike in July 2025 and ride most days: lunch rides during the
  week, longer rides on weekends and days off. I track rides
  privately on [Strava](https://www.strava.com/) and on [my workouts page](/workouts/).
- **Running:** Now that I've dropped some weight, I've started to mix in some running, especially trail running.
- **Calisthenics, core, and dumbbells:** variety several times a week.
- **Walking:** usually a couple of miles in the evening.
- **Other:** getting back into hiking, something I used to do
  [regularly](/photos/), plus mobility, yoga, and other assorted workouts here
  and there.

**Diet:**

- **No strict diet:** I haven't cut out any foods except sugary drinks, just
  made more deliberate choices. I still eat sweets, chips, burgers, pizza,
  bread, and ice cream, just less often and in smaller amounts.
- **Calorie counting:** logging everything in [Lose It!](https://www.loseit.com/). Using a basic food scale at home.
- **Fasting:** 16:8 baseline, usually closer to 18:6, with occasional 24-hour
  fasts and longer ones planned. I track fasts with Lose It. I do IF most days
  of the week.
- **Supplements:** a basic routine for general health, plus
  [SALTT](https://saltt.com/) drops for
  hydration and longer fasts.

**Habits:**

- **Nicotine:** quit. My tracked resting heart rate was about 15 bpm higher
  while I was using it.
- **Alcohol:** cutting back; still in progress.
- **Soda:** cut regular soda and sugary drinks a few years ago, ~~and most
  diet soda over the past year.~~ I've been enjoying Coke Zero and Dr. Pepper
  Zero lately, ugh.
- **Caffeine:** ~~cutting back, with tea in the mornings and nothing in the
  afternoon.~~ As of November 2026, I'm enjoying plenty of coffee and some diet
  soda throughout the day.

There's still progress to make, but these changes are meant to last. Things are
heading in the right direction, and I want to keep them there.
