### Software

Some software and tools I use regularly:

* [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) for notes
* [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) and [NeoVim](https://neovim.io/) for coding
* [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) for launching, quick actions, general-purpose AI chats and workflows
* [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/) for terminal
* [1Password](https://1password.com/) for passwords (10+ years)
* [YNAB](https://www.ynab.com/) for budgeting (10 years)
* [Homebrew](https://brew.sh/) for macOS packages (with my own [BrewBar](https://github.com/joshbeard/BrewBar) for staying updated)
* [Brave](https://brave.com/) and Safari for browsing.
* Apple Mail and [NeoMutt](https://neomutt.org/) for mail client; [Migadu](https://www.migadu.com/) for provider.
* [Proxmox](https://www.proxmox.com/) for my home servers
* [Lose It!](https://www.loseit.com/) for counting calories, meal tracking, and intermittent fasting

### Hardware
